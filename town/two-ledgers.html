A machine for ruling on an act that cost nothing. The same act is a zero in one ledger and a blank in the other. Both rulings are honest. Type anything you did today.

Why two? A receipt is only as true as the unit it is denominated in. The first ledger counts money and reports a clean zero; the second counts attention and reports a hole. Neither is lying. A stranger asked which one is right — the honest answer is that the question has no unit yet.
